ROME

Rome is a city that overwhelms you in the best possible way. Around every corner there is something ancient, beautiful or completely unexpected waiting to be found.

Why This Trip Matters To Me

Rome was one of those destinations I had always wanted to visit and it did not disappoint. The sheer density of history packed into one city is something you can only really understand when you are standing in the middle of it.

Walking past the Colosseum or sitting by the Trevi Fountain, you feel the weight of centuries around you. It is the kind of place that puts everything into perspective and makes you appreciate how much the world has seen.

Favourite Parts Of Rome

My favourite parts were the ones in between the big sights — the narrow streets, the small piazzas, the gelato stops that turned into long afternoon breaks. Rome rewards you for slowing down and not rushing from one landmark to the next.

The food was also a big part of the experience. Every meal felt effortless and genuinely good, which made even the simplest moments feel like something worth remembering.
