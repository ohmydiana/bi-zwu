DANCE

My dance journey started when I was 6 years old and had my first slovak folklore dancing class. Since then it has been a long ride of falling in and out of love with folklore, but as I was getting past my teenage resentment to everything, I quickly realised how much affection I had for it. Sadly, due to school and other responsibilities, I had to leave my dancing group after almost 13 years of dancing.

FS Mladosť

I am beyond grateful for all the years of my life I dedicated to dancing. I started in a children's dancing group and when I was 13, I transfered to FS Mladosť, dancing group for adults.

The years I spent in Mladosť were some of the most significant years of my life, I made great friendships and gained a whole lot of discipline and persistance. I don't think there's a feeling comparable with the feeling of being a part of a dancing group, that's why I'm incredibly thankful for this part of my life.

Calendar Photoshoot

In 2020, our dancing group, FS Mladosť, together with the photograph Michal Zahornacky and other talented staff, created the first edition of Herbarium Populi Vagi calendar for the year 2020. The project was created as a reflection on the concept that links traditional folk clothing with flowers, where the traditional garments are supposed to represent the flowers of our region - Váh.

The calendar itself, although it was the first edition, was a big success and won the award as the Most Beautiful Wall Calendar in Slovakia for 2020.
