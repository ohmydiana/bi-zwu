PHUKET

Phuket is one of those destinations that stays with you long after you leave. The colours, the warmth, the food and the energy of the place are unlike anywhere else.

Why This Trip Matters To Me

Phuket was my first experience of Southeast Asia and it completely changed how I think about travel. The distance from home, the completely different culture and the sheer beauty of the landscape made it feel like a genuinely transformative trip.

Everything about it felt new — the smells, the sounds, the food, the pace of life. It was one of those trips where you come back feeling like a slightly different person than when you left.

Favourite Parts Of Phuket

The beaches were obviously stunning, but what I loved most was exploring beyond them — the temples, the night markets, the street food stalls that appeared in the evening and turned the streets into something magical.

Thai food was also a revelation. Every meal was full of flavour and variety, and eating local rather than sticking to familiar options made the whole experience so much richer.
