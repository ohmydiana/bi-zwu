MALAGA

Malaga is the kind of place that gets under your skin without you even noticing. Sun, sea, good food and a relaxed pace that makes it very hard to leave.

Why This Trip Matters To Me

Malaga was one of those trips that felt effortless from start to finish. The weather, the food, the people — everything came together in a way that made it easy to just switch off and enjoy being somewhere new.

There is a warmth to the city that goes beyond the climate. It feels welcoming and unhurried, which made it one of the most enjoyable trips I have had in a long time.

Favourite Parts Of Malaga

The old town was my favourite area — wandering through the narrow streets, finding little tapas bars and stopping whenever something caught my eye. It never felt rushed or crowded, which made exploring it genuinely enjoyable.

The beach was also a big part of the trip. Spending an afternoon by the sea with good food nearby is hard to beat, and Malaga does it as well as anywhere I have been.
