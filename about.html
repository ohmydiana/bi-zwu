WHO AM I

I wear many roles in life — each one teaching me something different: a daughter, a sister, a girlfriend, an aunt, and, I'd like to think, a good friend to some. In my (still kind of short) life I already got to experience so many valuable things, got to see plenty of beautiful places and feel countless amazing feelings, all of which I'm super thankful for.

All throughout my life, I couldn't find the right box to fit myself in, which has been both my blessing and my curse. Curse, because of this constant desire to try new things, which is not always possible, so then the FOMO kicks in. Blessing, because of all the things I already got to try, whilst finding the right box for me.

Currently, however, the status I mostly wear is the one of a student. In 2024, I began my time at Czech Technical University in Prague, and that is my main concern right now. Scroll down a little to see more of my school history.

MY EDUCATION JOURNEY

This timeline represents my education journey all the way from the elementary school up until the university I attend now. Although there have been many times of momentary regret and breakdowns, I couldn't be more glad for all the people, knowledge and memories this journey gave me.

ZŠ CI Dubnica

2010 - 2019

The elementary school I attended. What I remember the most from my stay at this school are the friendships I made, because those are the strongest ones I have to this day.

Gymnázium Dubnica

2019 - 2023

As much as I hesitated to apply to this grammar school, it may have been one of the most important (good) decisions I've made in my life. The school prepared me well for my higher studies and gave me some of the most valuable things I have.

Avans UAS

2023 - 2024

I attended an International Business programme at this university, which I shortly realised wasn't the best fit for me. However, I am altogether very thankful for the experience it brought me.

FIT CTU

2024 - present

My current university, at which I am attending the Computer Science programme. So far, it has been a really bumpy ride at this school, considering it's very intense educational level.
